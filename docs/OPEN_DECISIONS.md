## CONFIRMED

- First implementation environment: Kaggle Notebook with GPU. Prove a single-video pipeline before eight-video processing, production modularization, UI, Docker, GPU VM deployment, or multi-GPU orchestration.
- YOLO26M is the target detector; Kaggle uses the Ultralytics Python API. Official `yolo26m.pt`, Ultralytics 8.4.175, the frame API, CPU smoke, and the selected TOR3-PAGI Kaggle `cuda:0` baseline are verified. Production dependencies will later be pinned in Docker; other runtime combinations and scenes are not established by this run.
- BoT-SORT is the target tracker; ReID-assisted identity association is part of the target architecture. Replacing BoT-SORT or removing ReID requires an explicit architecture decision, including after experiments.
- The camera stays at its recording location during each video; handheld shake/jitter is possible. It does not travel through the environment. Position/orientation may differ between videos.
- Initial geometry uses independently configured per-video pixel coordinates: Side A polygon, diagonal counting line, Side B polygon. No world-coordinate projection, homography, geographic projection, fixed angle, fixed orientation, or fixed dimensions are required.
- Reference example mapping confirmed by the user: A is upper-left of the yellow diagonal and approaching the camera; B is lower-right and moving away. This applies to that example only. Other videos require their own polygons/line and semantic metadata; no image-axis rule follows from the example.
- `A_TO_B` means origin in A → line crossing → arrival in B. `B_TO_A` is the reverse. Approaching/away labels are per-video semantic metadata, not inferred from X/Y or left/right/top/bottom motion.
- **TOR3-PAGI direction metadata:** [feedback 001](evidence/phase_5/feedback/G1_review_001.json) confirms `A_TO_B` = approaching the camera and `B_TO_A` = moving away for A-far/B-near; [feedback 002](evidence/phase_5/feedback/G1_review_002.json) retains that mapping and supplies concrete revision instructions. Presence in A/B alone establishes no movement direction. G1 remains historical/unapproved; engineer-adjusted G2 polygon outlines still need review. Other videos retain their own OPEN semantic mappings.
- **USER REQUIREMENT — G2 line/default template:** complete endpoints are `(4.5,516.5)` and `(936.4,305.4)`; adjust A/B polygons to the line and cap Side B at `X=875`, without shortening the line itself. The user requests this line as a reusable default and reliable behavior across videos. Native 1280 × 720 coordinate interpretation follows the existing G1 frame as an explicit visual-draft ASSUMPTION; resolution/aspect/viewpoint transfer and cross-video reliability are not established. Preserve per-video configurable geometry and do not present an untested default as universally correct.
- YOLO detects throughout the frame. Tracking, geometry eligibility, and counting are separate. Initial-frame objects may be tracked and establish an eligible origin, but count only after a subsequent valid crossing reaches the opposite side.
- Counting is derived from eligible persistent-track crossing events. Bounding-box contact is insufficient. Protect against jitter, trajectory fluctuation, temporary detection loss, re-entry, and noisy re-crossing; count once per valid event.
- **USER DECISION — anchor:** feedback 002 selects bottom-center (BC) for the current geometry. Its source box (linked raw measurement versus Kalman-filtered box), observation/prediction handling and demonstrated robustness remain OPEN; the selection is not an experiment result or physical road-contact guarantee.
- No formal ground-truth dataset exists. No accuracy, precision, recall, F1, MAPE, or counting-performance claim is justified by this specification or visual audit.
- Current authorization covers the completed Phase 2 baseline, cached Phase 3, TOR3-PAGI Phase 4 contribution test and the subsequently requested staged Phase 5 workflow: visual candidate → feedback → revision → rules → synthetic tests → temporal review → parameters. Phase 4 compared off/on using the byte-identical cache/video with CPU association, GMC none and actual CUDA external-encoder inference, without detector inference. Phase 5 now has exact revised line endpoints, B cap, engineer polygon-adjustment authorization and BC selection; [G2](evidence/phase_5/review_drafts/G2/README.md) remains the visual-revision stage before polygon/contract review and dependent validation. Do not skip those gates or claim all-video reliability. Later temporal replay uses retained ReID tracks without rerunning YOLO, tracker or encoder. No crossing algorithm, event generation, counting or crossing/noise/gap threshold selection has occurred. The user supplied Kaggle dataset mount `/kaggle/input/datasets/chrisbiran/traffic-tracker-videos`; GitHub remains source of truth. Stabilization/GMC, night enhancement and multi-GPU orchestration remain outside this task. Published detector/tracker defaults are untuned experiment configurations, not accepted product parameters.
